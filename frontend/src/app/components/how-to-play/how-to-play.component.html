How to Play

Fire Planner is a turn-based strategy game. Contain spreading wildfires before they burn too much of the forest.

Objective

Keep burned tiles below ~40–50% of the grid. Preserve as much forest (🌲) as possible and extinguish fires to maximize your score.

The Grid

Each tile is one of three states:

  • 🌲 Safe – healthy forest
  • 🔥 Fire – burning at intensity 1–3; the tile color deepens as intensity increases, so brighter/darker fire tiles are more dangerous and harder to extinguish
  • ⬛ Burned – lost forever

Each Turn

Each turn has two phases:

  1. Planning – spend your actions to place units, then click Next Turn.
  2. Resolution – actions apply, firefighters work, then fire spreads automatically.

Your Units

  • 🚒 Firefighter – place on a fire tile to reduce its intensity each turn. Persists for ~3 turns, then expires.
  • ✈️ Air Tanker – targets a 3×3 area and instantly reduces or removes fire. Great for covering a cluster quickly.

Actions & Economy

  • You get a fixed number of actions each turn.
  • Only one unit can occupy a tile at a time.
  • One unused action carries over to the next turn — use them wisely!

Scoring

  • ✅ Forest tiles preserved and fires extinguished earn points.
  • ❌ Burned tiles cost points.
  • ⭐ Bonus points for efficiency (unused actions) and surviving longer.

Game Over

The game ends if burned tiles exceed the threshold or the 5-minute time limit is reached.

Difficulty

  • Easy – 6×6 grid
  • Medium – 8×8 grid
  • Hard – 10×10 grid, faster fire spread

Tips

  • Watch tile colors — darker fire tiles are higher intensity and spread faster. Prioritize them before they get out of hand.
  • Use the air tanker on fire clusters; use firefighters to hold individual tiles.
  • Fire intensity grows each turn — act early before it reaches level 3.
  • Carrying over an action can save you in a critical turn.
